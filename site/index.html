Your HolyArrs server, on every screen.

Acolyte is an independent app for Android, iPhone and Apple TV. It talks straight to the HolyArrs server you host yourself: browse, search, ask for titles, follow downloads and look after your server. No cloud in between, no account with us.

Get test builds on GitHub Read the privacy policy

Acolyte is in testing and not in the App Store or Google Play yet. You need your own HolyArrs server; Acolyte is only the app, it does not include one.

What it does

The HolyArrs web app, laid out for your hand and your couch.

Acolyte follows the HolyArrs layout. What you see depends on your account and on which parts your server has switched on: managers get everything, everyone else gets browsing and requests.

  • Home and Explore

    Pick up where you left off, see what is new and popular, and browse by genre, cast member or collection.

  • Search

    Search your library and beyond, or limit the results to what you already own.

  • Library

    Movies, shows and music with filters you can save. Title pages bring seasons, episodes, cast and trailers.

  • Requests

    Ask for a movie or a show in a few taps and follow its status. Approvers can answer requests too.

  • Downloads and calendar

    Watch the queue, add links and files, and see what is due on a calendar.

  • Music

    Artists, albums and new releases, right next to your films and shows.

  • Live updates

    Screens refresh on their own while you look at them, using the live feed your server offers.

  • Manage your server

    For managers: users and roles, status and health checks, and more than 25 settings pages, from library folders and downloaders to alerts, backups and logs.

    Android and iPhone

  • Careful sign-in

    HolyArrs password with two-factor, Jellyfin, Emby, Plex (not on Apple TV) or an API key. Secrets are kept in the Android Keystore or the Apple Keychain.

Platforms

One app, three places to use it.

All logic is shared between the platforms, so a feature behaves the same everywhere. Each app is written natively for its system and follows its light and dark setting.

Your server, your data

Straight from your device to your server.

Acolyte has no backend of its own. It connects to the address you type in and to nothing in between. A few optional extras, such as trailers and some pictures, can reach other sites; the privacy policy lists every one of them.

Getting started

Three steps.

Once you have a server, connecting takes a minute.

  1. Run HolyArrs

    Set up your own server. The HolyArrs project explains how.

  2. Enter the address

    Type it the way you open HolyArrs in a browser, for example 192.168.1.10:6262 or media.example.com.

  3. Sign in

    Use the account you already have on the server. The support page covers the usual snags.
