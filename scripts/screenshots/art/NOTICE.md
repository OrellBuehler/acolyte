# Demo artwork

The posters, backdrops, title logos (transparent PNGs) and cast portraits in this folder are original artwork drawn by
`../make-art.py` (code in `../artgen/`) from shapes, gradients, noise and the open-licence fonts in `../fonts/`: layered
landscapes, skylines, planets, stage curtains and flat illustrations, a poster layout per title, and illustrated busts for the
cast. They show invented titles from `../catalog.py`; nothing here is taken from a film, series, poster, still or photograph, no
image was downloaded or generated by an AI service, and no title, studio, person or user name refers to a real work or person.
The generated files are released under the repository's MIT licence; the fonts keep their own licence (SIL OFL, see
`../fonts/`).

Re-running the script reproduces the files (fixed seeds).
