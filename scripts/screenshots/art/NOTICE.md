# Demo artwork

The posters, backdrops and title logos (transparent PNGs of the title in a system font) in this folder are original placeholder art generated by
`../make-art.py` (flat gradients, simple shapes, plain silhouettes for the cast and a title in a system serif font). They show
invented titles from `../catalog.py`; nothing here is taken from a film, series, poster or
still, and no title, person or user name refers to a real work or person. The generated files
are released under the repository's MIT licence.

No third-party artwork is used. (Free artwork such as the Blender open movies was considered
and left out: it would need a credit per image, and the generated set is easier to keep
consistent and impossible to confuse with a real release.)
